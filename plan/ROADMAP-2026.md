## What "done" looks like on Jan 3

- A weekly routine you've run for 14 weeks that you could keep running for 10 years
- Strength: goblet squat ~30 lb, RDL ~30 lb/hand, rows ~30 lb, 5 floor push-ups
- Cardio: 20-25 min continuous jog, or 45 min Zone 2 bike
- Pelvic floor: no leaking, no heaviness, under any load in the plan
- Core: side plank 45 sec, no doming under load
- A named 2027 goal with a date on it

## Where this goes in 2027

Realistic, if 2026 goes as written:
- **Q1:** first 5K back (March). Strength keeps climbing — this is the fastest gaining
  window you'll have.
- **Q2:** 10K. Bowflex loads into the 35-45 lb range on hinges and carries.
- **Q3:** half marathon is a reasonable target — Oakland in March or SF in July.
- **2028:** marathon, if you want it. Not before. Marathon training and a toddler is a
  real negotiation, and the base you build in 2027 is what makes it a choice instead
  of a gamble.

A note worth holding: lactation temporarily pulls mineral out of your bones — typically
3-5% over six months of breastfeeding — and it comes back after weaning, faster with
weight-bearing loading and adequate calcium. So the strength block running through
November and the wean in December are well matched by accident, and worth keeping that
way on purpose. The running will come back on its own; you already know how to do that.
The lifting is the genuinely new part, and it's the part with the 30-year payoff.
