# Phase 3 — The Mile *(revised)*
### PP weeks 15-16 · Mon Nov 23 → Sun Dec 6, 2026

**Two weeks, and they carry your headline goal.** This is the last block before work,
so the job is to get the running started and bank a strength baseline you can hold
through December.

You want to run a mile by Dec 31. If the readiness test passed, **you'll do it around
Dec 19** — with two weeks of buffer before your deadline.

## The walk/run ladder → the mile

**Only if you passed the readiness test.** Two runs a week, never consecutive days.
Ohlone Greenway — flat, paved, right there, and you told me you love getting outside.
That is not a small detail; it's the reason this will stick when the strength work
doesn't.

| Week | Dates | Session |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Nov 23-29 | 6 × (1 min jog / 2 min walk) |
| 16 | Nov 30 - Dec 6 | 6 × (2 min jog / 2 min walk) |
| 17 | Dec 7-13 | 5 × (3 min jog / 90 sec walk) |
| 18 | Dec 14-20 | 3 × (5 min jog / 2 min walk) → **the mile, Sat Dec 19** |
| 19-20 | Dec 21 - Jan 3 | 12-15 min continuous, easy |

**The mile itself:** warm up with 5 minutes of walking, then run one mile without
stopping, at a pace where you could hold a conversation. No watch target. The only
success criterion is that you finish it and feel fine the next day.

**Rules that are not optional:**
- Easy pace. Full sentences, or you walk.
- **The 24-hour check.** Leaking, heaviness, or spotting in the day after means the
  last step was too big. Repeat the week instead of advancing.
- Never progress duration and pace in the same week.
- **Run after a feed or pump**, and get a sports bra refitted now and again after you
  wean. Your chest changes size twice in eight weeks.
- New shoes, gait-fitted. Fleet Feet or Transports in the East Bay. Pregnancy can
  permanently change foot size and arch — treat it as a first fitting, not a repeat of
  2013 or 2017.

**Didn't pass the test?** Nothing is wrong. Keep the impact ladder, retest in December,
and use the two slots for incline treadmill walking. The mile moves to January, and
January is a better month for it anyway.

## Strength

Hold the format, add a little load. **3 × 10-12 at RPE 7-8.** Record your numbers on
squat, RDL, row and press before Dec 6 — those become the floor you protect through
December and the launch point in January.
