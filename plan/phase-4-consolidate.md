# Phase 4 — Work & Wean
### PP weeks 17-20 · Mon Dec 7, 2026 → Sun Jan 3, 2027

Four weeks in which you go back to work, finish weaning, and get through the holidays.
Three genuinely hard transitions stacked on top of each other, in the darkest month of
the year, on a body that is also recalibrating its hormones.

**The goal is explicitly not progress. It is to arrive on Jan 4 having not stopped.**

This is the phase people lose. They hold a five-session week as the standard, miss it,
feel like they've failed, and quit — then spend January rebuilding what they had in
November. The way you don't is by lowering the bar on purpose, *in advance*, so that
what you actually do counts as success rather than as falling short.

## Minimum effective dose

**2 strength + 2 runs + The Reset daily.** That is the whole target. Hit it all four
weeks and Phase 4 is a complete success — no qualifier.

**The 25-minute full-body session.** Five movements, no accessories, done:
goblet squat 3×10 · single-arm DB row 3×10 · DB floor press 3×10 · RDL 3×10 ·
side plank 3×30 sec/side.

**Hold your Dec 6 loads. Do not chase anything this month.** Maintaining strength takes
roughly a third of the work that building it does — two sessions a week at your
existing loads will hold everything you built, and trying for more while sleep-deprived
and newly back at work is how you get hurt or quit.

**Travel version, zero equipment:** sit-to-stand 3×12 · push-up 3×max · single-leg RDL
3×10/side · reverse lunge 3×8/side · side plank 3×30 sec/side · 20 min walk.

## The three things happening at once

### 1. Back at work
Pick your two strength days and two run days **before** your first day back, and put
them in the calendar as appointments. A session that isn't scheduled doesn't happen in
December. The Metro510 gym is your best asset here — it removes the commute from the
workout, and 25 minutes downstairs is a real session.

If mornings collapse, move everything to lunch or right after work. Do not try to
protect a 6am slot on four hours of sleep and a commute; protect the *count*, not the
time of day.

### 2. Weaning — the tail end
By Dec 7 you should be at zero or close to it, having started Oct 26. Full detail in [`../nutrition/WEANING-AND-RETURN-TO-WORK.md`](../nutrition/WEANING-AND-RETURN-TO-WORK.md).
The training-relevant parts:

- **Your calorie needs drop by roughly 450-500/day as the milk goes.** Taper your intake
  alongside the taper, not all at once at the end. This is the single most common way
  people gain weight after weaning.
- **Expect a mood dip** in the week or two around the last sessions — prolactin and
  oxytocin fall before estrogen fully rebounds. It is hormonal, it is temporary, and it
  is worth knowing is coming rather than reading it as "I can't handle going back."
- **Connective tissue firms up post-wean.** Relaxin-related laxity fades, which is
  genuinely good for the run ladder. January running will feel more stable than
  December running.
- **Bone density recovers after weaning**, and loading speeds that up. Keep the two
  strength sessions and the 1,000-1,300 mg of calcium going through and past the wean.

### 3. Holidays
Week of **Dec 28 is a deload** regardless: same schedule, ~60% of the load, one fewer
set. You will feel better on Jan 4 for having done it.

## The one rule for December

**Never zero.** Missing a session is normal this month. Missing a *week* is what
breaks a routine. On the worst day, The Reset and a 10-minute walk is a Red-tier
session and it counts — see the three-tier day in
[`PROGRESSION-RULES.md`](PROGRESSION-RULES.md).

## End-of-2026 review — do this on Jan 3

Write it into `logs/2026-review.md`:

1. What can you do now that you couldn't on Sep 8?
2. Which sessions did you consistently skip, and what was actually in the way?
3. Top loads on squat, RDL, row, press — Dec 6 vs. Jan 3.
4. Pelvic floor: leaking, heaviness, anything, under any load?
5. Running: where did you land?
6. How did the wean go — energy, mood, appetite, weight?
7. What did you eat on your best-energy weeks that you didn't on your worst?
8. **What is the one goal for 2027?** Name it precisely and put a date on it.

## Then what?

Jan 4, 2027 starts the forever template — the same 3:2:1:1 shape, 4-week blocks, deload
every fourth week, indefinitely. Full rules in [`PROGRESSION-RULES.md`](PROGRESSION-RULES.md).

January is your real build month: weaned, settled at work, baby around 4.5 months,
appetite and hormones stabilizing, and four months of foundation underneath you. It
will be the fastest progress you make all year. December's only job is to deliver you
there intact.
