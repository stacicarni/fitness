# Phase 4 — Consolidate
### PP weeks 19-20 · Mon Dec 21, 2026 → Sun Jan 3, 2027

Two weeks across the holidays. The goal is explicitly **not** progress. It is to
arrive on Jan 4 having not stopped.

Almost everyone loses their routine here and then spends January rebuilding what they
had in December. The way you don't is by lowering the bar on purpose, in advance,
rather than missing a high bar and calling it a failure.

## Minimum effective dose

**2 strength + 2 cardio + The Reset daily.** That's it. If you hit that both weeks,
Phase 4 is a complete success.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon | Strength — full body, 25 min (see below) |
| Tue | Run/walk or bike, 20-25 min |
| Wed | Reset only |
| Thu | Strength — full body, 25 min |
| Fri | Reset + walk |
| Sat | Run/walk or long walk |
| Sun | Rest |

**The 25-minute full-body session:** goblet squat 3×8 · DB row 3×8 · DB floor press
3×8 · RDL 3×8 · farmer carry 3×40 sec. Five movements, no accessories, done.

**Travel version, zero equipment:** sit-to-stand 3×12 · push-up 3×max · single-leg
RDL 3×10/side · reverse lunge 3×8/side · side plank 3×30 sec/side · 20 min walk.

## Week of Dec 28: deload

Keep the schedule, cut every load by ~40%, cut sets by one. You will feel better on
Jan 4 for having done it. Deloads are not lost time; they are when the adaptation
from the previous eight weeks actually lands.

## End-of-2026 review — do this on Jan 3

Write the answers into `logs/2026-review.md`:

1. What can you do now that you couldn't on Sep 8?
2. Which sessions did you consistently skip, and what was actually in the way?
3. What were your top loads on squat, RDL, row, press?
4. Pelvic floor status — leaking, heaviness, anything, under any load?
5. Running status — where did you land?
6. What did you eat on your best-energy weeks that you didn't on your worst?
7. **What is the one goal for 2027?** Name it precisely and put a date on it.

## Then what?

Jan 4, 2027 starts the forever template — the same 3:2:1:1 shape, run in 4-week
blocks with a deload every 4th week, indefinitely. The full rules are in
[`PROGRESSION-RULES.md`](PROGRESSION-RULES.md). Nothing new gets invented. You just
keep turning the same crank, and it works for the next decade.
