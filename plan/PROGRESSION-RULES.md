# Progression Rules — the engine

The plan is four phases. **This file is the part that lasts.** Once you know these
six rules you can write your own training forever and never need a program again.

---

## Rule 1 — Double progression

Every exercise has a rep range (say 3 × 8-10).

1. Start at the **bottom** of the range with a load you could beat by 2 reps.
2. Each session, add reps until you hit the **top** of the range on **every set**.
3. Hit the top on all sets for **two sessions in a row** → add load, drop back to the
   bottom of the range, repeat.

Load increments: **+2.5 lb** for presses, rows, and anything single-arm. **+5 lb** for
squats, RDLs, and carries. The Bowflex increments in 2.5s up to 25 lb, then 5s — that
maps cleanly onto this.

Example: goblet squat 3×8 @ 15 → 3×9 @ 15 → 3×10 @ 15 → 3×10 @ 15 again → 3×8 @ 20.
That's ~4-5 weeks per 5 lb. Slower than it sounds; faster than it feels. In a year it
is a different body.

## Rule 2 — RPE ceiling by phase

RPE = reps in reserve. RPE 7 means you could have done 3 more.

| Phase | Ceiling | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | RPE 3-4 | Should feel pointless |
| 1 | RPE 5-6 | Working, but you finish fresh |
| 2 | RPE 7 | Last 2 reps are real work |
| 3+ | RPE 7-8 | Hard, form still clean |
| Ever | **Never RPE 10** | Grinding reps postpartum buys nothing and costs a lot |

## Rule 3 — Deload every 4th week

Weeks 1-3 build, week 4 backs off: **same movements, ~60% of the load, one fewer set.**
Non-negotiable, and it applies for the rest of your life. Deloads are when adaptation
consolidates. Skipping them is how people plateau at month 5 and quit at month 6.

Deload weeks in this plan: **Oct 19, Nov 16, and Dec 28.**

## Rule 4 — The symptom traffic light 🔴

This outranks every other rule in this repo.

| Signal | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Urine leaking, any amount | Too much load or too much impact | Stop the exercise. Regress one level next session. |
| Heaviness / dragging / bulging | Pelvic floor is fatigued or overloaded | Stop the session. Legs up the wall 10 min. Regress a full week. |
| Abdominal doming or coning | Pressure exceeds what the midline can manage | Shorten the range until it disappears. That range is your limit. |
| Bleeding increases or turns red again | You did too much | Back to the last symptom-free volume for 4-5 days. |
| Pubic bone / SI joint pain | Pelvic instability | Drop wide-stance and single-leg work. Narrow stance only. |
| Any pelvic pain during a lift | Not a "push through" | Stop. If it repeats, see the PT before continuing. |

**Regressing is the program working.** The plan is a hypothesis; your body returns the
result. Nothing here is a failure of discipline.

## Rule 5 — The three-tier day

You have a newborn. Some days the plan is fiction. Decide which tier you're in
*before* you start, so a hard day doesn't turn into a skipped week.

| Tier | When | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | Slept 6+ hrs (broken is fine), feel decent | The full session as written |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | Slept 4-6 hrs, functional but flat | Half the sets, same movements, same loads. **Never cut load — cut volume.** |
| 🔴 **Red** | Slept <4 hrs, sick, or emotionally underwater | The Reset + a 10 min walk. That's the whole workout, and it counts. |

A Red day done is worth more than a Green day skipped. Consistency compounds;
intensity doesn't.

## Rule 6 — Never add two things at once

New load *or* new reps *or* new duration *or* a new movement. One variable per week.
When something goes wrong you'll know exactly which change caused it — that's the
entire point.

## The forever template (2027 onward)

```
Mon   Strength A  — Squat + Push
Tue   Cardio      — run or bike
Wed   Strength B  — Hinge + Pull
Thu   Mobility    — Peloton yoga
Fri   Strength C  — Full body + carries
Sat   Long cardio — long run or long walk
Sun   Rest        — The Reset
```

Every session covers some of: **squat · hinge · push · pull · carry · single-leg · core.**
That's the whole checklist. If a week contains all seven patterns and a deload every
fourth week, you are training well. Everything else is detail.

Run it in 4-week blocks: 3 build weeks + 1 deload. Every 12 weeks, pick a new emphasis
(strength, running volume, body composition — one at a time). Retest the same lifts
each block so progress is a number, not a feeling.
