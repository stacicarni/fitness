# Phase 1 — Rebuild the Base
### PP weeks 7-10 · Mon Sep 28 → Sun Oct 25, 2026

**Requires:** Phase 0 exit checklist complete + provider clearance.

**Goal:** establish the weekly shape you'll keep for years — 3 strength, 2 cardio,
1 mobility, 1 rest — at loads that are deliberately too easy. You are buying
consistency and tissue tolerance, not adaptation. Adaptation is Phase 2's job.

## The weekly template (this shape never changes again)

| Day | Session | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Strength A — Squat + Push | 30-35 min |
| **Tue** | Bike Zone 2 + walk | 20-25 min bike |
| **Wed** | Strength B — Hinge + Pull | 30-35 min |
| **Thu** | Peloton yoga / mobility + walk | 20-30 min |
| **Fri** | Strength C — Full body + carries | 30-35 min |
| **Sat** | Long walk 40-60 min, or elliptical 20 min | 40-60 min |
| **Sun** | Rest + The Reset | 12 min |

Sessions are movable. The *ratio* is what matters: **3 strength : 2 cardio : 1 mobility
: 1 rest.** Never two strength days back to back in this phase.

**Every set: exhale on the effort, pelvic floor lifts with it.** That habit built now
is what lets you load a 50 lb dumbbell in the spring without leaking.

**Why so many carries?** They train the deep core and pelvic floor under real load in
the exact position you spend your life in right now — upright, holding something heavy,
on one side. Carries transfer to motherhood better than any core exercise on a mat.

## Cardio in Phase 1

**Tuesday — Peloton bike, Zone 2.** 20 min in Week 7, +2-3 min/week to 30 min by
Week 10. Zone 2 = you can hold a full conversation; roughly 60-70% of max HR, or
about 108-126 bpm for you. Peloton's "Low Impact" and "Endurance" rides fit; ignore
the leaderboard entirely, it is not for this phase.

**Saturday — long walk 40-60 min,** or 20 min elliptical in the Metro510 gym if
weather is bad. The elliptical is a good bridge: it gives you a running-like gait
pattern with zero impact.

**Still no running.** Not on the treadmill either. The treadmill is for walking at an
incline, which is genuinely useful — 15 min at 3.0 mph / 6% grade is real work with
zero impact.

---

## Load selection, concretely

Set the Bowflex to the lowest number where the **last 2 reps of a set feel like work**
but your form and breathing don't change. That is RPE 5-6, and it is where you live
this phase. If you finish a set and think "I could have done 10 more," go up one
setting next session.

Starting points to try in Week 7 — adjust from there:
- Goblet squat: **10 lb**
- RDL: **12.5 lb each**
- Row: **15 lb**
- Floor press: **12.5 lb each**
- Farmer carry: **15 lb each**

The 2 × 3 lb weights aren't useless — they're right for lateral raises, external
rotations, and split squat additions where 5 lb is a big jump.
