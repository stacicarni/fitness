# Phase 1 — Rebuild the Base *(revised for symptoms + goals)*
### PP weeks 7-10 · Wed Sep 30 → Sun Oct 25, 2026

**Requires:** the Sep 29 visit and clearance.

**Goal:** establish the six-day shape you keep for years, at loads deliberately too
easy. You committed to 6 sessions × 30 min. That's ambitious with a newborn and an
older child, so the week is built as **4 non-negotiable + 2 bonus** — hit four and the
week counts, hit six and you're ahead. This protects you from the pattern where a
6-day target gets missed on Thursday and abandoned by Saturday.

## Two standing constraints from your intake

**No loaded carries until a PT clears the heaviness.** They're the best exercise in the
program for most people and the worst one for you right now.

**You said you're not interested in lifting heavy, so we won't.** Strength here is
moderate load, 10-15 reps, circuit-flavored — closer to the Orange Theory format you
already know you show up for than to a powerlifting template. You'll still get
meaningfully stronger; progressive overload works fine in this rep range. The Bowflex
goes to 52.5 and you will likely never need past 25.

## Cardio

**The bike is your engine this phase, not walking.** It's seated, non-weight-bearing,
and it doesn't provoke the heaviness. It's also the fastest route to the aerobic base
you need for the mile in December.

- **Tue:** 20 min Zone 2, building to 30 by week 10. Zone 2 = full conversation,
  roughly 110-130 bpm for you. Peloton "Low Impact" or "Endurance."
- **Sat:** 25-30 min, same effort.
- **Walks:** keep them at your symptom-free length, split across the day. Progress by
  3-5 min only after 5 symptom-free days.

**No running, and no treadmill running.** Incline walking on the treadmill is fine and
useful once heaviness allows — 15 min at 3.0 mph and 6% grade is real work with zero
impact.

## Load selection

Set the Bowflex to the lowest number where the **last two reps feel like work** but
your form and breathing don't change. That's RPE 5-6, and it's where you live this
phase. If a set finishes and you think "I could do ten more," go up one notch next time.

You'll likely land around 10-15 lb for most things by week 10, and that's correct. The
2 × 3 lb weights are genuinely useful for lateral raises and external rotations, where
5 lb is a big jump.
