# Exercise Library

Every movement in the plan, with the cue that matters and how it progresses.
**Universal rule: exhale on the effort, pelvic floor lifts with it.**

## Breath & pelvic floor

**360° breathing** — Hands on lower ribs. Inhale: ribs widen sideways and back, belly
softens, pelvic floor releases down. Exhale: everything returns without gripping.
*Wrong:* belly-only breathing, or sucking in.

**Connection breath** — Inhale to release. Exhale: gently lift pelvic floor, low belly
draws in behind it. **Effort 3/10.** *Progress:* supine → sidelying → seated → standing
→ during exercise.

**Pelvic floor quick flicks / long holds** — 10 fast; then 5 holds building 3→10 sec.
Full release between every rep. Once daily, not more.

## Squat pattern

**Sit-to-stand** → **Goblet squat to box** → **Goblet squat** → **Double-DB squat**
Cue: weight through mid-foot, knees track over toes, chest up, exhale on the way up.
*Progress:* deeper box → no box → load.

**Wall sit** — Thighs toward parallel, ribs stacked over hips. *Progress:* time, then
single-leg.

## Hinge pattern

**Hip hinge, unloaded** → **DB RDL** → **Single-leg RDL**
Cue: push hips *back*, not down. Shins vertical. Bar path close to legs. Stop where
your hamstrings limit you, not where your back rounds. Exhale to stand.
*This is the pattern for lifting the car seat.* Practice it there too.

**Sumo DB deadlift** — Feet wide, one DB between them. Easier on the low back, more
glute. Good entry point.

**Glute bridge** → **DB glute bridge** → **Bridge march** → **Single-leg bridge**
Cue: ribs down, push through heels, squeeze glutes at the top, exhale up. Don't arch
the low back to get higher.

## Push pattern

**Wall push-up** → **Counter push-up** → **Bench/box push-up** → **Floor push-up**
Cue: hands under shoulders, elbows ~45°, body in one line, ribs down.
*Progress by lowering the surface,* not by dropping to knees — the incline keeps the
plank position honest.

**DB floor press** — Lying on the floor, elbows stop at the ground. Safer than a bench
press without a spotter, and the floor limits range while shoulders are re-stabilizing.

**DB overhead press** (Phase 2+) — Seated or standing, ribs down, don't arch. Exhale up.
*Wait until the exhale-and-lift is automatic — overhead pressing raises intra-abdominal
pressure more than most lifts.*

## Pull pattern

**Band row** → **Single-arm DB row** → **Chest-supported / bent-over row**
Cue: pull the elbow back and toward the hip, shoulder blade moves first, don't rotate
the torso. Squeeze for one count.

**Band pull-apart / face pull** — Shoulder blade health, and it directly counteracts
the forward-rounded feeding posture. Do these liberally; they're basically free.

## Single-leg

**Split squat** (both feet down) → **Rear-foot-elevated / Bulgarian** → **Loaded**
**Step-up** — low box → higher box → loaded. Push through the *top* foot, don't push
off the bottom one.
**Single-leg balance** — eyes open → eyes closed → on a pillow. 30 sec/side.
**Single-leg calf raise** — the sleeper test for running readiness. Build to 25/side.

## Carries — the most underrated movement here

**Farmer carry** (both hands) — Stand tall, ribs down, breathe normally, don't hold
your breath. Walk 30-45 sec.
**Suitcase carry** (one hand) — Resist the side-bend. This is an anti-lateral-flexion
core exercise disguised as walking. It is exactly the pattern of carrying a car seat.
*Progress:* load, then time.

## Core

**Heel slides** → **Dead bug, heel taps** → **Dead bug, full extension** → **Dead bug
with band pull-over**
Cue: low back stays lightly connected to the floor. Exhale on the extension. **Stop at
the range where the belly domes.**

**Bird dog** — opposite arm and leg, slow. Hips level — put a water bottle on your low
back if you're not sure. No doming.

**Side plank from knees** → **from feet** → **with top-leg lift**
The best-tolerated core exercise postpartum. Loads the obliques and the deep system
without the pressure spike of a front plank.

**Pallof press** (Phase 3) — Band anchored at chest height beside you; press straight
out and resist the rotation. Anti-rotation core, directly transferable to carrying a
baby on one hip.

**Copenhagen plank, from the knee** (Phase 2+) — adductors. Common weak link for
returning runners.

**Not in this library, on purpose:** crunches, sit-ups, V-ups, bicycles, boat pose,
double leg lowers, full front planks before Phase 3, mountain climbers. Each spikes
intra-abdominal pressure in a way that's a poor trade this year. You are not missing
anything — carries and loaded compounds train the core harder.

## Impact ladder (Phase 2)

Heel raise-and-drop → pogo hops (both feet) → lateral hops → single-leg hops in place
→ forward bounds → jog in place → running-man drill.
**One level per week, only if symptom-free.**

## Cardio

**Walking** — the primary Phase 0 modality and permanently underrated. Flat, then
graded. Ohlone Greenway.
**Peloton bike** — Zone 2 (full-sentence conversation) → intervals in Phase 2. Ignore
the leaderboard. Saddle comfort is the postpartum gate, not fitness.
**Elliptical** (Metro510) — running gait, zero impact. Good bridge before running.
**Treadmill** — walking at 4-8% incline is a genuinely hard workout with no impact.
Save actual running for Phase 3.

## Peloton app — what to search, by phase

| Phase | Use | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Postnatal · Restorative Yoga · Stretching · Meditation | Strength, Core, Bootcamp, Power Yoga, all Cardio |
| 1 | + Low Impact rides · Slow Flow · Beginner Strength (for form only) | Bootcamp, HIIT, Core, Jump-based |
| 2 | + Power Zone rides · Intervals · Full Body Strength | Plyo/jump classes, Core classes with crunches |
| 3 | + most Strength · Yoga Flow · Tread walk/run | Anything that makes you hold your breath |

Peloton's own strength classes rarely progress load the way this plan does — use them
for movement quality, technique, and on Yellow-tier days. The written strength days
are where the progression lives.
